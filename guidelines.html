Rules & Guidelines

Everything you need to know before your Badol adventure.

Camp Rules

Rules & Guidelines

Reservations Only

No walk-in campers, hikers, or bikers allowed. All guests must register in advance.

One Group at a Time

Only 1 group is allowed to camp at a time to preserve a private, peaceful experience.

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in: 2:00 PM. Check-out: 2:00 PM (1 day = 24 hours).

No Motor Vehicles on Trail

Motor bikes are not allowed to enter the trail to the campsite. We promote maintenance of the serenity and pristine condition of the natural environment.

Walk-in Restrictions

Management and barangay officials have the right to deny walk-in visitors entry if registered guests are already at the campsite.

Waiver Required

All guests must accomplish a waiver with full disclosure of any medical conditions. Clients with conditions need a medical clearance certificate.

Be Prepared

What to Expect

Cool Mountain Weather

Expect cold nights at 1,400 MASL. Bring warm clothing, sleeping bags, and extra layers.

6 km Mountain Trail

The campsite requires a hike from the jump-off point. Wear proper hiking shoes with good grip.

Limited Electricity

Bring fully charged power banks and flashlights. The campsite is off-grid.

Possible Leeches (Matek)

Creek trails may have leeches especially during the rainy season. Wear long pants and use insect repellent.

Unpredictable Weather

Mountain weather can change quickly. Be prepared with rain gear and waterproof bags.

Limited Signal

Mobile signal may be weak in the area. Enjoy the digital detox! Inform loved ones of your trip beforehand.
