Facilities & Activities

The Badol Experience

Explore hidden waterfalls, swim in crystal-clear waters, and reconnect with nature.

Amenities

Campsite Facilities

Water & Restrooms

Restrooms and shower rooms are available at the campsite. Plus, there is plenty of mountain spring water for drinking and washing.

Tents & Camping

Pitch your tent or set up a hammock anywhere within the forested camping area. Tents and hammocks are also available for rent.

Exclusive: Only 1 group is allowed to camp at a time.

Food & Kitchen

Whether you choose to cook your food in the kitchen/grilling station, or avail of our food assistance, we have you covered.

Bonfire Area

Gather around the campfire with friends and family under a blanket of Benguet stars at 1,400 MASL.

Adventures

Activities

Trekking Within the Campsite

  • Creek Trail — Trek upon the Liteng Creek where up to 4 waterfalls can be visited, 3 of which feature safe, crystal-clear pools you can swim in.
  • Liteng Waterfalls Viewpoint (Manpuy-as) — View the majestic waterfalls of Liteng, Kapangan.

Trekking in Nearby Areas

  • Camp Utopia** — Visit the historic campgrounds of the 66th Infantry Battalion which fought against the Japanese in World War 2.
  • Camp Utopia View Deck** — Get a glimpse of the majority of the Municipality of Kapangan, Halsema Highway, and Tublay.
  • Sammy's Peak — Enjoy the view from one of the highest points of Mount Mugong. A portion of the West Philippine Sea and La Union could be seen.

** Coordinate with Sagubo LGUs

Sunset & Sunrise Viewing

  • Sunrise viewing at the campsite
  • Sunset and sunrise viewing at Sammy's Peak

Swimming

Swim and bathe in the cool waters of Liteng Creek. It is just a few meters away from the campsite.

Tree Planting / Re-potting

  • Tree nursery — Re-potting of Pine tree saplings
  • Tree planting — Pine trees, Mahogany, Acacia, Fruit-bearing trees (Lemon, Guava, etc.)
