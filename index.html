Welcome to Badol

A Sanctuary Above the Clouds

Nestled deep in the pine-covered mountains of Kapangan, Benguet, Badol Camping Grounds offers a pristine escape at 1,400 meters above sea level. Covering 5,000 sqm of lush forested land, the campsite is a haven where the air is crisp, the skies are vast, and the trails lead to hidden waterfalls and breathtaking panoramic views of the Cordillera highlands.

We strive to boost physical and mental wellness through nature and self-attunement activities in a self-sustained, community-involved environment — so that we and future generations can live the best we can on the best natural environment the only Earth we have has to offer.

Exclusive Camping: We guarantee maximum privacy by allowing only 1 group to camp at a time.
